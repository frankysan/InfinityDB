An open-source community reference for Infinity

Know your options.

InfinityDB brings official Infinity Army data and N5.3 rules references together so you can move between armies, profiles, Fireteams, Equipment, Weapons, Skills, Traits, States, Hacking Programs, and related rules without having to piece those connections together yourself.

01

Explore the game data you already use

Browse units by Army, profile, availability, Skill, Equipment, and Weapon, or inspect an Army's Fireteam chart directly. Unit pages bring together profiles, loadouts, AVA, characteristics, and Army-specific relationships, while the reference pages let you approach the same information from the rules or equipment side.

02

Follow rules instead of hunting for them

Skills, Equipment, Traits, States, and Hacking Programs include concise summaries, links to source material, and related rules where the connection is clear. The goal is not to replace the rules, but to make relevant information easier to find and follow.

03

Know where information comes from

InfinityDB keeps official Army information, rules references, and project-added explanations distinguishable. When the available sources are incomplete or ambiguous, that uncertainty should remain visible instead of being presented as certainty.

Where the project is going

A more connected Infinity reference.

The aim is to make units, Armies, Fireteams, Skills, Equipment, Weapons, Traits, States, Hacking Programs, and related rules easy to follow from one another. Current work focuses on clearer presentation, consistent navigation, better search and discovery, and broader coverage of the current N5 rules.

What guides InfinityDB

Three principles

Accuracy

InfinityDB aims to reflect official sources faithfully. When information is incomplete, version-specific, or ambiguous, that uncertainty should remain visible rather than being silently guessed away.

Connectivity

Related information should be easy to follow. InfinityDB links rules and game information when the connection is clear, while avoiding links that would imply more than the rules support.

Transparency

Privacy policy

Private by default.

No visitor profiles

InfinityDB does not require an account, assign persistent visitor identifiers, or use advertising trackers, fingerprinting, or visitor-level analytics. Operational monitoring is aggregate-only and excludes IP/geolocation, user-agent/fingerprint, referrer, cookie/session/preference values, query/search terms, and per-user navigation histories.

Browser settings stay yours

Settings use browser sessionStorage by default and InfinityDB does not use localStorage. Persistent first-party cookies are created only after you enable Remember settings and accept the confirmation. They contain settings values only, last for up to one year, and are deleted when Remember settings is turned off.

Shareable state is explicit

Search and filter state may be encoded in a URL so it can be bookmarked or shared; that URL can therefore also appear in your browser history or wherever you choose to share it. InfinityDB excludes those query/search values from its aggregate metrics. Hosting/network infrastructure still has to process connection metadata to deliver the site, and external links follow the destination site's own privacy policy.
