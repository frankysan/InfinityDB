Player reference

A clearer way to explore Infinity

Know your options.

InfinityDB is a practical reference for the moments between inspiration and deployment. Find the units available to an army, understand what they bring, and keep the details you need close at hand.

01

Start with your army

Choose an army to narrow the catalog to the troops you can actually field. It is an easy starting point whether you are learning a faction or revisiting an old favourite.

02

Find a unit quickly

Search by name when a profile catches your eye, then open it to see its available loadouts, stats, equipment, skills, and where it can be used.

03

Compare choices with context

Profiles are grouped by army so you can spot the options and differences that matter to your list. Optional units can be included when you want the broader picture.

Built for the table

Less hunting. More planning.

Use InfinityDB while you sketch a list, check a profile before a game, or settle a question with your opponent. Distances can be shown in centimetres or inches, so the reference meets your table where it is.
