Player reference

A local reference for Infinity

Know your options.

InfinityDB is a web app that turns an Infinity Army data snapshot into a clear, searchable reference. It is made for the time between an idea and a game: exploring an army, checking a profile, and understanding the rules and equipment that shape a list.

01

Explore what you can field

Choose an army to narrow the unit catalog to the troops it contains. Search by name, include optional availability such as mercenaries or reinforcements when useful, and open a unit to see the profiles and loadouts available in each army.

02

Follow details to their source

Unit pages collect general and army-specific profiles, statistics, AVA, skills, equipment, weapons, and loadouts in one place. The Skills, Equipment, and Weapons catalogs then let you work backwards from a rule item to the units and loadouts that use it.

03

Built from a checked snapshot

InfinityDB merges and normalizes downloaded Army data, validates it, and imports it into a local SQLite database. The browser reads that database through a small, read-only API, keeping the source snapshot, the data pipeline, and the reference interface clearly separated.

Purpose and direction

Less hunting. More planning.

Today, InfinityDB is a fast reference for units and their connected rules data, with centimetre and inch distance preferences for the table. Its goal is to grow into a more complete and dependable way to explore Infinity data: make relationships easier to understand, surface more useful context, and add capabilities only where they remain transparent, accurate, and useful to players.
