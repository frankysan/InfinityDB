A local reference for Infinity

Know your options.

InfinityDB turns Infinity Army snapshots and API metadata into a clear, searchable reference. It is made for the time between an idea and a game: exploring an army, checking a profile, and understanding the rules, equipment, weapons, and traits that shape a list.

01

Explore what you can field

Choose an army to narrow the unit catalog to the troops it contains. Search by name or filter by skills, equipment, and weapons; include optional availability such as mercenaries or reinforcements when useful; then open a unit to see the profiles and loadouts available in each army.

02

Follow details to their source

Unit pages collect general and army-specific profiles, statistics, AVA, skills, equipment, weapons, and loadouts in one place. Skills, Equipment, and Weapons catalogs let you work backwards from an item to the units and loadouts that use it, while Traits adds concise summaries and shows where shared special rules apply.

03

Data-driven from official sources

InfinityDB derives its reference data from official Infinity Army snapshots and API metadata. It merges, normalizes, and validates those sources before importing them into a local SQLite database, so the browser can present connected game data through a small, read-only API.

Purpose and direction

Less hunting. More planning.

InfinityDB is growing into a better companion for choosing, collecting, and playing your army. Alongside fast unit and rules reference, including the first concise trait summaries, the roadmap includes fuller summaries for skills, equipment, ammunition, and remaining traits; Fireteam eligibility and reference pages; model imagery; and a personal collection tracker. Every addition should help you spend less time hunting through data and more time making informed choices for the table.

How InfinityDB is built

Three principles

Accuracy

InfinityDB uses official data sources and strives to represent them as accurately as possible. When a source is incomplete or ambiguous, that uncertainty should remain visible.

Flexibility

InfinityDB should make its data easy to browse in more ways over time while staying simple, fast, and customizable to the way you plan and play.
